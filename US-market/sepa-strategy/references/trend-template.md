# Trend Template — 8 Mandatory Conditions

The trend template is a pre-entry qualification filter. All 8 conditions must be satisfied simultaneously. If any condition fails, skip the stock entirely.

## MA Staircase (Conditions 1–5)

These five conditions establish a healthy, stacked bullish moving average alignment.

**Condition 1: Price > 150MA AND Price > 200MA**
Confirms the stock is in a long-term uptrend.

**Condition 2: 150MA > 200MA**
The 150-day MA must be above the 200-day MA — bullish MA hierarchy.

**Condition 3: 200MA trending up for at least 1 month (ideally 4–5 months)**
Compare today's 200MA with the value from 1 month ago. It should be higher. This confirms the long-term trend is healthy, not a temporary bounce.

**Condition 4: 50MA > 150MA AND 50MA > 200MA**
Short-term moving average leads the pack — strong recent momentum.

**Condition 5: Price > 50MA**
Confirms near-term momentum is positive.

**Complete MA hierarchy:** Price > 50MA > 150MA > 200MA, with 200MA sloping upward.

## Price Position (Conditions 6–7)

**Condition 6: Price ≥ 30% above 52-week low**
Proves the stock has truly left its bottom. Calculate: `(Current Price / 52-Week Low − 1) × 100%`.

**Condition 7: Price within 25% of 52-week high**
The stock should be trading near its highs. Calculate: `(1 − Current Price / 52-Week High) × 100%`. Must be ≤ 25%.

## Relative Strength (Condition 8)

**Condition 8: RS ranking ≥ 70th percentile (prefer 85–90+)**
Only trade true market leaders. RS measures 12-month price performance vs the entire market. Top 15% (RS > 85) are real leaders.

Sources: IBD RS Rating, MarketSmith, TradingView "Relative Strength" indicator, or calculate manually vs S&P 500.

## Memory Aid

1. **MA Staircase** (1–5): Price > 50MA > 150MA > 200MA, with 200MA rising
2. **Price Position** (6–7): Far from the low (≥30%), near the high (≤25% away)
3. **Relative Strength** (8): Market leader, RS > 70th percentile
