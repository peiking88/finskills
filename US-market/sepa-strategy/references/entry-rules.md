# Entry Point Rules

The "Specific Entry Point" is the core of the SEPA name. This is about entering at a very specific price level with defined risk.

## The Pivot Point

Below the pivot, supply equals or exceeds demand. Above the pivot, demand overwhelms remaining supply. The pivot = the highest price within the consolidation pattern.

## Buy Zone: Pivot to +5%

- **Valid entry window:** From the pivot price to 5% above the pivot
- **Beyond +5%:** Do NOT enter. Stop distance stays the same but profit potential shrinks — risk/reward deteriorates
- **Missed it?** Wait for the next consolidation and breakout

## Volume Confirmation

| Breakout Volume vs 20-Day Average | Interpretation |
|-----------------------------------|---------------|
| ≥ 2.0x | Strong institutional buying — high confidence |
| ≥ 1.5x | Standard confirmation — normal entry |
| 1.2x–1.5x | Marginal — enter with caution, tight stop |
| < 1.2x | Insufficient — high probability of false breakout |

## True Breakout vs False Breakout

**True breakout:** Volume spike ≥ 1.5x average, stock closes near day's high, VDU preceded breakout, follow-through continues next day.

**False breakout:** Weak volume, stock touches pivot but closes back below, no VDU preceded the attempt, long upper wick on breakout candle.

## Alternative Entry: Pocket Pivot (Advanced)

Earlier entry during consolidation:
- **Trigger:** Up-day volume exceeds volume of any down day in the previous 10 sessions
- **Entry:** Near 10MA or 20MA within the consolidation
- **Stop:** 1–2% below pocket pivot day's low (tighter than standard)
- Appropriate for experienced traders only

## Five Entry Rules (Iron Laws)

1. Buy within 0–5% of the pivot point
2. Never chase beyond 5% above the pivot
3. Never enter during consolidation without a pocket pivot signal
4. Be cautious if breakout volume is below 1.5x average
5. Avoid entering within 2 weeks of an earnings report

## Risk/Reward Validation

```
R/R Ratio = (Target Price − Entry Price) / (Entry Price − Stop Price)
```

- Minimum: 2:1
- Preferred: 3:1 or better
- If < 2:1: Do not take the trade
