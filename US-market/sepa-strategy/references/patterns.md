# Consolidation Patterns

All SEPA patterns share the same entry logic: **breakout above the pivot point + volume confirmation ≥ 1.5x 20-day average**.

## VCP (Volatility Contraction Pattern) — The Core Pattern

Minervini's signature pattern. Think of price as a spring being compressed: each pullback compresses it tighter. When the spring reaches maximum compression (supply exhaustion), it releases forcefully.

### 7 Identification Rules

1. **Stage 2 uptrend (prerequisite):** Price above 50MA/150MA/200MA with bullish alignment
2. **Pullback depths decrease in sequence:** Typical: 20% → 12% → 6% → 3%. Each contraction roughly 20–30% smaller. Minimum 3 contractions; 4–5 is ideal
3. **Volume shrinks in sync, ending with "Volume Dry-Up" (VDU):** Volume drops to multi-week low — supply exhaustion
4. **Higher lows:** Each pullback bottom is higher than the previous
5. **Clear pivot point:** The high of the consolidation range = resistance
6. **RS > 70** (preferably 85–90+): Ensures the stock is a genuine market leader
7. **Market in bull or neutral environment:** Major indices above their MAs

### Quality VCP vs Fake VCP

**Quality VCP:**
- Pullback depths strictly decreasing
- Each low higher than the previous
- Volume decreasing with each pullback
- Clear VDU in final contraction
- Breakout with strong volume (≥ 1.5x average)

**Fake VCP (traps):**
- Irregular pullback depths
- Lows not progressively higher
- Volume expanding on declines
- Only 2 contractions (insufficient structure)
- Breakout with weak volume (below average)

## Cup with Handle

- **Cup:** U-shaped recovery, depth 12–35% from peak to trough. Must be U-shaped (rounded), not V-shaped
- **Handle:** Small pullback after cup completes, ≤ 1/3 of cup depth (typically ≤ 12%)
- **Volume:** Low at cup bottom, even lower during handle, large on breakout
- **Duration:** 7–65 weeks total
- **Pivot:** Top of the handle's range

## Flat Base

- **Depth:** ≤ 15% from high to low
- **Duration:** 5–10 weeks
- **Volume:** Contracts during consolidation, expands on breakout
- **Pivot:** Top of the flat range

Often appears in stocks too strong to pull back much. The tighter the range, the better.

## Bull Flag

- **Flagpole:** Sharp advance of 25%+
- **Flag:** Slight downward drift, pullback ≤ 50% of flagpole
- **Volume:** Shrinking during flag; expansion on breakout
- **Duration:** 1–5 weeks for the flag portion
- **Pivot:** Top of the flag range

## High Tight Flag (Rarest and Most Powerful)

- **Prerequisite:** Stock must have already advanced 100%+ in 4–8 weeks
- **Flag:** Pullback ≤ 25% from peak, extremely tight
- **Volume:** Extremely dry during flag; massive on breakout
- **Duration:** 1–4 weeks for the flag
- Rare but highest success rate
