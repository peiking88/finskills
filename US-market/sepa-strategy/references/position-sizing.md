# Position Sizing, Stop Loss & Pyramiding

The most critical part of SEPA. "Not losing big is the only prerequisite for winning big." You cannot control how much a stock goes up, but you can fully control how much you lose.

## Position Size Formula

Determine the maximum dollar amount you're willing to lose, then work backward:

```
Shares = (Account Value × Risk Per Trade %) ÷ (Entry Price − Stop Price)
```

### Worked Example ($100,000 account, 1% risk)

1. Max loss = $100,000 × 1% = **$1,000**
2. Entry: $50.00. Stop at −7% = $46.50. Stop distance = **$3.50/share**
3. Shares = $1,000 ÷ $3.50 = **285 shares**
4. Total position = 285 × $50 = **$14,250** (14.25% of account)
5. Target 1: $54.00 (+8%, sell half). Target 2: $57.50 (+15%, sell 25%)
6. R/R = $7.50 / $3.50 ≈ **2.14:1**

## Stop Loss Three-Phase Evolution

### Phase 1: Initial Hard Stop (At Entry)
- Set immediately: **entry price minus 7–8%**
- Non-negotiable. If triggered, exit immediately.

### Phase 2: Move to Breakeven (At +8% Profit)
- Sell half to lock in profit
- Move stop up to entry price (breakeven)
- Remaining half is now a "free trade"

### Phase 3: Trailing Stop (At +15% Profit)
- Sell another 25%
- Trail remaining 25% using the 20-day MA
- Update stop weekly to 1–2% below current 20MA
- When price closes below 20MA, exit all remaining

### Special Case: Rapid Advance
If stock surges 20–25% quickly, tighten stop to below 10MA.

| Scenario | Stop Placement |
|----------|---------------|
| At entry | Entry price − 7–8% |
| Stock at +8% | Entry price (breakeven) |
| Stock at +15% | 1–2% below 20MA |
| Rapid surge (+20–25%) | Below 10MA |
| Close below 50MA | Serious warning — consider full exit |

## Iron Rules

1. Stops only move UP, never down
2. Never average down on a losing position
3. After 3–4 consecutive losses, reduce risk to 0.5% and cut positions
4. Average loss should be 4–5%, hard cap at 10%

## Pyramiding (Adding to Winners)

Add to winning positions with decreasing size:

| Tranche | Timing | Size | Price (Example) | Shares |
|---------|--------|------|----------------|--------|
| 1st (Main) | VCP breakout at pivot | 50% | $50.00 | 100 |
| 2nd (Add) | +8%, pullback to 20MA | 30% | $54.00 | 60 |
| 3rd (Add) | Next base breakout | 20% | $58.00 | 35 |

Largest position at lowest cost = minimum risk, maximum cushion. Each addition has a new breakout signal confirming the trend.

## Handling Losing Trades

SEPA wins only ~50–55% of the time. The win/loss size ratio matters more than win rate:
- Win trades average +15–30%
- Lose trades average −5–7%
- Over 10 trades at 50% win rate: 5 × 15% − 5 × 6% = +45% net

### Loss Review (Three Questions)

1. **Execution or strategy problem?** — Fix the habit or study more examples
2. **"Good loss" or "bad loss"?** — Good: followed all rules. Bad: broke rules
3. **Individual stock or overall market?** — If breakouts are frequently failing, check market environment first
