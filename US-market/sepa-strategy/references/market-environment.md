# Market Environment Assessment

The market environment is the master switch for all SEPA activity. Even the best individual stock setups fail at high rates in bear markets.

## Three Market Environments

### Bull Market (Indices Strong)

**Identification:**
- S&P 500 and Nasdaq above their 200-day moving averages
- Market breadth expanding (more stocks advancing than declining)
- New 52-week highs consistently outnumber new 52-week lows
- Breakouts generally follow through

**SEPA parameters:**
- Risk per trade: 1–2% of account
- Position size: S-tier setups 10–15%, A-tier 5–10%
- Max concurrent positions: 6–8
- Strategy: Aggressive offense

### Choppy / Sideways Market (Direction Unclear)

**Identification:**
- Indices oscillating without clear direction
- Frequent failed breakouts
- Roughly equal advancing and declining stocks
- Mixed sector signals

**SEPA parameters:**
- Risk per trade: 0.5–1% of account
- Position size: Only A+ grade setups, half normal size
- Max concurrent positions: 2–3
- Strategy: Cautious observation

### Bear Market (Sustained Decline)

**Identification:**
- Major indices below their 200-day moving averages
- More than 50% of stocks trading below their 200-day MAs
- New 52-week lows consistently > new 52-week highs
- Quality breakouts fail or reverse quickly
- Defensive sectors outperforming growth

**SEPA parameters:**
- Risk per trade: 0% (no new positions)
- Gradually exit to 100% cash
- Strategy: Preserve capital. Wait for the next bull market.

## Quick Environment Check

1. **S&P 500 vs 200MA** — above = bullish, below = bearish
2. **Nasdaq vs 200MA** — tech sector health
3. **Advance/Decline line** — broadening = healthy; narrowing = deteriorating
4. **New Highs vs New Lows** — consistent new highs > new lows = bull
5. **VIX level** — sustained above 25–30 suggests elevated fear
6. **Recent breakout success rate** — if last 5 breakouts all failed, the market is likely the problem

## Bull to Bear Transition (Gradual)

1. Leading stocks start failing on breakouts
2. More stocks hitting 52-week lows
3. Indices spending more time below 50MA
4. Former leaders break below 50MA, then 200MA
5. Market rallies on decreasing volume
6. Indices breach 200MA

**Response:** At each step, gradually reduce exposure. Don't wait for full bear confirmation. By the time everyone agrees it's a bear market, the damage is already done.
